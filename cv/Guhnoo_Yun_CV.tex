\cvsection{Research Statement}
% looser lines for easier reading; ragged right avoids stretched word gaps
{\linespread{1.3}\selectfont\raggedright
\textbf{Analyzing and designing the inner workings of AI perception models to improve how
machines see and understand the real world.}\enspace AI perception models can work well long before anyone knows why. My research explores the underlying mechanisms, often by treating each core
component inside a model as a filter and analyzing what it keeps or removes, from broad patterns to fine details, as information passes through it. I then turn that understanding into better~designs.
\begin{itemize}[itemsep=5pt, topsep=6pt]
  \item Analyzed convolution and attention as spectral filters, and designed new components that balance
        fine details and broad patterns. Then grounded them in theory, and the resulting models outperformed leading vision architectures. \rt{1, 4}
  \item Viewed linear attention as a fixed-size memory, whose cost grows only in proportion to input size, and used a filter to decide what that memory keeps and recalls. The result is a general-purpose
        operation that works across image classification, object detection, segmentation, and
        image generation. \rt{11}
  \item Developed vision algorithms that detect falls and recognize daily activities for care
        robots at home and in hospital isolation wards. \rt{5, 6, 8, 9} Built the vision part of
        systems pairing first-person video with brain signals, using foundation models and large
        language model agents to describe where a person is and what they came to do. \rt{2, 3}
\end{itemize}
Looking ahead, I aim to unify the different mathematical views of AI perception models, such as filters and memories, into a single framework that brings machines closer to human intelligence.
My work already builds on both of these views. With this framework, I will design models that
combine video, sound, and body signals, and that run well on the chips inside real machines such
as humanoid robots. The goal is machines that understand the real world, built on designs we can~explain.\par}
